Flagged ("the app," "we," "us") is published by **[COMPANY / DEVELOPER LEGAL NAME]** ("[LEGAL
NAME]"). This policy explains how the app handles information. The short version: **Flagged is
designed to work entirely on your device and does not collect, transmit, or sell your personal
data.**

## 1. Our privacy approach

Flagged is an offline food-label scanner. Reading labels, matching ingredients, saving your
profiles and Safe List, and tracking your scan stats all happen **locally on your device**. We do
not operate servers that receive your content, and we do not require an account.

## 2. Information we do NOT collect

- We do **not** collect your name, email, or contact details. (A first name you optionally enter in
  Settings is stored only on your device to personalize the greeting.)
- We do **not** upload the photos you scan or the text recognized from labels.
- We do **not** track you across other apps or websites, and we include no advertising trackers.
- We do **not** sell or share personal information.

## 3. Camera

The app uses your device camera **only** to read the text on food labels, and this processing
happens **on-device**. Captured images and recognized text are used locally to produce your scan
results. Thumbnails you choose to save to your Pantry are stored **on your device** and are marked
to be excluded from device backups. You can revoke camera access at any time in your device
settings; the camera-dependent features will then be unavailable.

## 4. Information stored on your device

The following are stored **locally** and are not sent to us:

- Your filter profiles (selected Quick Packs, category and ingredient toggles, custom ingredients).
- Your Pantry / Safe List items, including any packaging thumbnail and the saved ingredient text.
- Your scan statistics (labels read, red flags caught, clean scans) and free-trial count.
- App settings (e.g., your first name, onboarding completion).

This data remains under your control and is removed if you delete the app (subject to your device's
backup settings).

## 5. Purchases

Flagged offers an auto-renewing annual subscription to unlock unlimited scanning. Purchases are processed by
**Apple** or **Google**, and entitlement status is managed via **RevenueCat**. We do not receive
your full payment details. These providers process limited purchase and device/identifier
information as needed to complete and validate the purchase and to restore it across your devices.
See:

- Apple Privacy Policy: `https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/`
- Google Privacy Policy: `https://policies.google.com/privacy`
- RevenueCat Privacy Policy: `https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy/`

## 6. Children's privacy

Flagged is not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction)
and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. Because the app collects no
personal data, no such information is gathered in any case.

## 7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (e.g., under GDPR or CCPA/CPRA), you may have rights regarding personal
data. Because Flagged does not collect personal data on our servers, we generally hold no personal
data to access, correct, or delete. Data stored on your device is managed by you through the app
and your device settings. For questions, contact us below.

## 8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Material changes will be reflected by updating the
"Last updated" date and, where appropriate, an in-app notice. If we ever introduce analytics,
crash reporting, or any data transmission, we will update this policy first.
